Privacy Policy & SMS Terms

Alkali Systems — LOTO / SWP Permit Management

What we collect

We collect your name, email address, phone number, and job title when you set up your account. This information is used solely to operate the internal permit management system and to send you SMS notifications about permits and signature requests you are involved in.

How we use your phone number

Your phone number is used only to send transactional SMS alerts related to your work on this system — such as permit approvals, signature requests, and LOTO status changes. We do not share your phone number with third parties, use it for marketing, or sell it.

Opting in

SMS notifications are optional. You can opt in by checking "Receive SMS notifications" in your profile settings. Message frequency varies based on permit activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

You can opt out at any time by:

  • Unchecking "Receive SMS notifications" in your profile, or
  • Replying STOP to any SMS you receive from us.

After texting STOP you will receive one confirmation message and no further SMS will be sent.

Help

Text HELP for help. For questions about this policy contact your system administrator.
